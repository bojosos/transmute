Alchemy GPU formats

Every core WebGPU texture format and every WebGL 2 sized internal format, each through the paths a page uses: upload, copy back (WebGPU), sampling with texel fetches compared with a CPU decode of the uploaded bytes, and, where the format allows it, clearing it as a render target and storing to it from a compute shader. 4x4 textures, every texel checked. Written for Alchemy (no network). The verdict goes into document.title (Alchemy GPU formats: PASS or Alchemy GPU formats: FAIL <test>) and body[data-verdict]; --dump-dom waits for it (at most 150 s).
