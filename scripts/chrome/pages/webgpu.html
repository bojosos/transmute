Alchemy WebGPU smoke

A WebGPU smoke written for Alchemy: adapter and device, a compute pass whose storage buffer is read back through mapAsync, an indexed, instanced render pass with a uniform buffer, a sampled texture and a vertex buffer into an offscreen texture (read back), and a render pass into a canvas through getCurrentTexture() (copied out and read back). Every step runs inside a validation error scope and under a time limit. The verdict goes into document.title (Alchemy WebGPU: PASS or Alchemy WebGPU: FAIL <test>) and into body[data-verdict]. --dump-dom waits for it: a chain of failing image loads holds the load event until the verdict is set (at most 90 s).
