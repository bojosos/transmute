Alchemy WebAssembly

An inline, hand-assembled WebAssembly module: add, mul, fill (writes linear memory), sum (a checksum loop over linear memory) and dispatch (a call through a table). It is compiled with new WebAssembly.Module and instantiated synchronously; sum is then called until hot so V8 tiers it up from Liftoff to TurboFan. WebAssembly.instantiate is also called; its result lands in #async if it resolves before the dump. Without WebAssembly (V8's jitless mode) the page says so.
