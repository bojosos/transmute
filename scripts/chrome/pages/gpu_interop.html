Alchemy GPU interop

How real pages move pixels between the web platform's surfaces: images, accelerated 2D canvases, video, WebGL and WebGPU canvases, ImageBitmaps, VideoFrames and workers. Written for Alchemy (no network: images are made by a canvas, video by a canvas capture stream). Each test reads the result back and compares it with what it drew. The verdict goes into document.title (Alchemy GPU interop: PASS or Alchemy GPU interop: FAIL <test>) and body[data-verdict]; --dump-dom waits for it (at most 150 s).
