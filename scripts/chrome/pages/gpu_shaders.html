Alchemy GPU shaders

The shading languages real pages write, one feature group per test: GLSL ES 3.00 through WebGL 2 (which ANGLE translates to Metal Shading Language) and WGSL through WebGPU (which Tint translates). Built-in functions, integer and bit semantics, packing, matrices, control flow, structs, arrays, pointers, atomics, texture functions and stage built-ins. Each test computes 16 vec4 results on the GPU (a 4x4 float render target or a storage buffer) and compares them with the same computation in JavaScript. Written for Alchemy (no network). The verdict goes into document.title (Alchemy GPU shaders: PASS or Alchemy GPU shaders: FAIL <test>) and body[data-verdict]; --dump-dom waits for it (at most 150 s).
