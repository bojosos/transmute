Alchemy GPU report

The page-side equivalent of chrome://gpu: WebGL and WebGL 2 strings (through WEBGL_debug_renderer_info), extensions, limits and context attributes, and the WebGPU adapter's info, features and limits, each classified as hardware, software (SwiftShader, a fallback adapter) or unavailable. The classification is in body[data-webgl], [data-webgl2], [data-webgpu] and in document.title: Alchemy GPU report: HW when all three are hardware, otherwise Alchemy GPU report: webgl=... webgl2=... webgpu=.... --dump-dom waits for the WebGPU part (a chain of failing image loads holds the load event, at most 150 s).
